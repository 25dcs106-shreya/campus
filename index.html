A campus built for engineers

CampusHub University runs undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across Computer Engineering, Information Technology, AI & ML, Electronics, Mechanical and Civil Engineering. The campus has dedicated labs for networking, robotics and software development, a 24-hour library, hostels for over 4,000 students, and an on-campus health centre and sports complex.

Recruiters that visit every year include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant and a growing list of product-based startups through the placement cell.
